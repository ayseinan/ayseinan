# Hi, I'm Ayşe Keleş 👋

**MSCA Postdoctoral Fellow at the [University of Galway](https://www.universityofgalway.ie/)**, Ireland.
I work on **interpretable AI for medical imaging**: segmentation and classification of pediatric brain tumours in MRI, explainable AI and clinical decision support.

- 🔬 Current project: [DL-I-PBraTSC](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101152004), Deep Learning Based Interpretable Pediatric Brain Tumors Segmentation and Classification (Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions)
- 🎓 PhD in Computer Science; previously at Ankara Medipol University
- 🏛️ Industry background: Computer Engineer at the Ministry of Health, Türkiye (databases, decision support systems, software project management)
